Why Client-Side Privacy Matters for Contractors and Freelancers

When you run an independent consultancy or contracting business, your time tracking data contains some of your most sensitive operational intelligence: confidential client names, project scope details, internal billable rates, and precise daily activity logs.

1. Eliminating Cloud Security Exposure

Traditional SaaS time tracking applications transmit every keystroke, timer update, and project label to a centralized server. While convenient, this architecture inherently introduces risk. Third-party cloud breaches, database misconfigurations, or employee leaks can expose your confidential client lists and financial earnings.

TimeDoco takes a radically different approach: 100% client-side execution. Stored strictly in browser IndexedDB, your data remains physically confined to your machine.

2. Zero Reliance on Cloud Uptime

When a cloud SaaS vendor experiences an outage or API breakdown, your workflow grinds to a halt. As a Progressive Web App (PWA), TimeDoco works completely offline. Whether you are on an airplane, in a low-connectivity zone, or working through cloud provider outages, your timers and reports function flawlessly.

3. Total Data Ownership

Cloud services frequently enforce vendor lock-in or charge monthly fees to access historical data. With TimeDoco, you own your raw data in full. You can export complete JSON backups at any time, run offline PDF/CSV reports, and migrate seamlessly across devices without paying subscription fees.
