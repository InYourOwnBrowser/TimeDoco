About TimeDoco

I initially built TimeDoco to solve a personal headache: keeping an accurate record of my hours and navigating a complex maze of timesheet codes at work. As I refined the app for my own daily use, I realized this lightweight, privacy-focused approach would be incredibly useful for freelancers, consultants, and contractors, too.

Instead of keeping it to myself, I decided to release it as a free web app. I’m always eager to hear how it’s working for you—please feel free to reach out with feedback, your specific use cases, or any feature enhancements you’d like to see added in the future!
