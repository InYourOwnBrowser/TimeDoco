## The Basics: Are Freelancers Subject to GDPR?

Yes. Under Article 2 of the GDPR, any self-employed individual or small business that processes personal data relating to identifiable living individuals in the European Economic Area (EEA) must comply with GDPR principles—regardless of whether your business is physically located inside or outside the EU.

### Data Controller vs. Data Processor
* **Data Controller:** The entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data (e.g., your client, or you when managing your own client records).
* **Data Processor:** An entity that processes personal data on behalf of a controller (e.g., a cloud software vendor or third-party SaaS host).

When you log time for a client, your timer descriptions and task notes frequently contain **Personally Identifiable Information (PII)**:
* Client contact names and email addresses.
* Customer support ticket summaries containing end-user details.
* Codebase references, medical project details, or legal case notes.

## How Cloud Time Trackers Create Hidden GDPR Risks

When you enter task notes into a standard cloud time tracking app (such as Toggl or Clockify), you are transferring that data from your device to a cloud SaaS provider.

Under GDPR rules, this transaction triggers several legal obligations:

### 1. The Sub-processor Requirement (Article 28)
When you send client data to a third-party cloud tracker, that cloud platform becomes a **Data Sub-processor**. Under GDPR Article 28, you must execute a legally binding **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** with that provider. If you use a free cloud tool without a formal DPA, you may be operating in technical violation of EU data protection law.

### 2. Cross-Border Data Transfer Restrictions
Many cloud time tracking services host primary databases on servers located outside the EU (such as in the United States). Transferring EU personal data across borders requires approved transfer mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses or EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification). If your SaaS vendor lacks compliant transfer certifications, you bear legal liability as the data controller.

### 3. Data Breach Notification Obligations (Article 33)
If your cloud time tracking vendor suffers a data breach exposing user time logs, GDPR Article 33 requires notification to supervisory authorities within **72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach. Identifying which client data was exposed in a third-party SaaS breach can be extremely difficult.
