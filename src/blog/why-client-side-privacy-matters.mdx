If you freelance or consult, your time tracking data is highly sensitive. You are logging confidential client names, project scopes, and your actual billable rates.

When I was looking for a tracker for my own data science and software projects, handing my clients' information over to a third party just felt wrong. Almost every SaaS app sends all this data to a central server. I built TimeDoco to work differently.

## 1. Zero Cloud Exposure
Most trackers log every timer update and project label to the cloud, making your data vulnerable to breaches or server misconfigurations. TimeDoco runs entirely client-side. Your data lives in your browser's IndexedDB, physically staying on your machine.

## 2. It Actually Works Offline
Cloud outages break workflows. Because TimeDoco is a PWA, it doesn't care if your internet drops or a server goes down. You can log time on a flight or during a connectivity blackout, and everything works perfectly.

## 3. You Own Your Data
I hate vendor lock-in, which is why the web app is completely free. You can export a JSON backup, generate PDFs, or pull CSVs whenever you want without hitting a paywall.

Try it out. Your data stays with you, and it doesn't cost a thing.
