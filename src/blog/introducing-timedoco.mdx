## The Problem I Was Trying to Solve

I built TimeDoco because I was completely fed up with the mainstream time tracking tools on the market. Between my primary work as a data scientist and managing software engineering projects on the side, keeping an accurate, reliable log of my billable hours was a constant source of friction.

When you look at popular tools like Toggl Track, Harvest, or Clockify, they all follow the same standard SaaS blueprint:

1. **Mandatory Account Registration & Data Lock-in:** Before you can track a single minute, you must sign up for an account, verify an email address, and send every detail of your workday—client names, project descriptions, internal rates, and work schedules—to an external cloud server.
2. **Subscription Creep & Gated Features:** Basic capabilities like unrounded timesheet exports, custom billing rates, historical reporting, or team tags are routinely locked behind recurring monthly subscriptions ($9 to $20+ per user per month).
3. **Cloud Outages & Internet Dependency:** If your internet connection drops, or if the SaaS provider experiences a server outage, your timer stops working or fails to save your latest entries.
4. **Data Aggregation & Surveillance:** Cloud services frequently aggregate user metrics, monetize platform data, or subject your sensitive client billing logs to third-party data breaches.

I did not want another bloated SaaS subscription, nor did I want my confidential client data living on a remote server. I wanted a **privacy-first time tracker without an account**—a clean, responsive tool that got out of my way, ran entirely on my own device, and allowed me to export professional reports in seconds. When I could not find a solution that fit these requirements, I built TimeDoco.

## How TimeDoco Works Under the Hood

TimeDoco is designed from the ground up as a **100% client-side web application and Progressive Web App (PWA)**. Here is what makes this architectural choice fundamentally different from traditional cloud-based time trackers:

### 1. Client-Side Data Storage via IndexedDB
Instead of making network API calls to a centralized database every time you start or pause a timer, TimeDoco reads and writes directly to your browser's structured IndexedDB storage. Your data never leaves your device. There are no background analytics, no telemetry scripts, and no third-party tracking pixels monitoring your billable tasks.

### 2. Full Offline Capability & Resilient Timers
Whether you are working on a flight, in a coffee shop with spotty Wi-Fi, or during an ISP outage, TimeDoco operates with full functionality offline. Timers use absolute Unix timestamps rather than volatile background JavaScript intervals. Even if you accidentally close your browser tab or restart your computer, your active running timer will recalculate exact elapsed duration the moment you reopen the app.

### 3. Total Data Ownership & Portability
Because your time logs are stored locally, you own them completely. TimeDoco provides a single-click JSON Backup and Restore engine in Settings. You can create complete encrypted local backups, migrate your full dataset across browsers or devices, and restore your history at any time without hitting a paywall or requesting data export permissions from a support team.

### Key Workflow Features

* **Instant Timer & Fast Manual Entry:** Start a live timer with one click or log manual hours retroactively. Need to log flat-fee project deliverables or non-time expenses (like domain purchases or software licenses)? TimeDoco natively supports fixed-cost overrides alongside hourly entries.
* **Flexible Grouping & Timecodes:** Organize your work with customizable groups (e.g., Client A, Internal R&D, Side Projects) and granular timecodes. Set default hourly rates per timecode to automatically compute gross earnings.
* **Overrun Audio Alerts & Task Estimates:** Assign expected duration minutes to specific tasks. TimeDoco continuously compares running elapsed time against your targets and plays an optional audio alert chime when an estimate is exceeded.
* **Entry Splitting & Precision Editing:** Easily split a continuous block of logged time into separate task segments when your focus shifts mid-afternoon.
* **Timesheet Matrix & Calendar Views:** View your time through adaptive timeline visualizations, weekly timesheet matrixes, and interactive calendar grids to analyze productivity patterns over time.
* **Multi-Currency & Custom Tax Handling:** Set global or per-report currencies and configure inclusive or exclusive tax percentages (e.g., VAT, GST, Sales Tax) to render accurate invoice totals on generated PDF reports.

## Client-Ready Reporting: PDF, CSV, and ICS Export

Generating client reports in TimeDoco is fast, flexible, and completely private. In the **Analysis & Reports** view, you can filter entries by date range, client group, or timecode, and immediately generate export files:

1. **Professional PDF Reports:** Produce formatted PDF summaries complete with your business logo, client contact headers, tax breakdowns, custom reference fields (such as PO Numbers or Tax Registration IDs), and detailed line-item tables.
2. **Unrounded CSV Exports:** Download raw timesheet records with exact decimal durations (e.g., 1.425 hours) for seamless import into accounting software like QuickBooks, Xero, or custom spreadsheet templates.
3. **ICS Calendar Feeds:** Export standard iCalendar (.ics) files to visualize your completed work blocks directly inside Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Microsoft Outlook.

## Why TimeDoco Is Free

A common question people ask about client-side software is: *If there is no monthly subscription, how is this project sustained?*

The answer comes back to architecture. Traditional SaaS time trackers incur massive monthly infrastructure costs maintaining cloud servers, managed relational databases, authentication systems, and security monitoring teams. Because TimeDoco runs 100% locally inside your web browser, my server costs are practically zero—limited to static hosting and domain management.

This means TimeDoco can remain **100% free for standard web app usage**. There are no artificial limits on the number of entries, projects, clients, or PDF exports you can generate.
