## The Problem I Was Trying to Solve

I built TimeDoco because I was completely fed up with the time trackers currently on the market. Between my work as a data scientist and managing various software development projects on the side, keeping an accurate record of my time was a constant struggle.

Most tools out there are either incredibly bloated, uncomfortably intrusive with how they handle personal data, or they simply lack the flexibility needed to make time logging seamless. I wanted a tracker that got out of my way rather than adding another layer of admin to my day. When I could not find a straightforward solution that respected my privacy and fit my workflow, I decided to build it myself.

## What TimeDoco Does

TimeDoco is a straightforward and reliable time tracking application. It is designed to let you focus on your actual work while capturing exactly what you need to invoice clients, track your productivity, or review your week.

Here is what you can expect:

* **Privacy First:** All your data stays right where it belongs. I built this with a strict focus on client-side privacy.
* **Powerful Reporting:** You can generate clean timesheet matrixes and calendar views to visualize exactly where your week went.
* **Tax and Currency Tools:** Built-in guides and reporting for taxes and multiple currencies take the headache out of invoicing.
* **Flexible Grouping:** Use timecodes, grouping management, and project templates to organize your entries exactly how your brain works.

## Who This Is For

I created this package for freelancers, developers, consultants, and anyone else who needs to measure their hours accurately. If you value your privacy, prefer keeping your data local, and just want a clean interface that does the heavy lifting for you, TimeDoco is built for you.

## My Favorite Features

I designed a few specific tools in here that I genuinely love using every day:

* **Manual Entry and Fixed Costs:** Let us be real, sometimes you just forget to hit start, or you are billing a flat rate for a project. Adding manual entries for your hours or dropping in a fixed cost is incredibly fast and easy.
* **Split and Edit Entries:** Sometimes a single block of work covers two completely different tasks. The entry split and edit modals let you cleanly divide your logged time after the fact.
* **Global Active Timer Bar:** You can always see exactly what is running without having to dig through menus.
* **Backup Reminders:** Since your data lives entirely on your device, the app displays friendly backup reminder banners so you never lose your history.

## Give It a Try
